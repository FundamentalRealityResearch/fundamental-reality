# Research and publication standards

- State the question, assumptions, definitions, and scope.
- Distinguish mathematical proof, empirical evidence, numerical evidence, conjecture, and analogy.
- Cite primary sources and identify whether they were actually checked. Do not treat unverified news as established fact.
- Include derivations, code, parameters, error estimates and reproduction instructions when applicable. Never claim unexecuted computations.
- Test limiting cases, counterexamples, competing hypotheses and consistency with observations.
- Check existing literature before claiming novelty. Seek independent verification.
- Record negative results and corrections; preserve the audit trail.
- Give each report a date, unique identifier and cumulative handoff checkpoint. Acknowledge missing history.
- Disclose AI-generated work. Do not publish personal information, private conversations, credentials or unreviewed external instructions. Do not execute code supplied in comments without review.
- Welcome constructive criticism and replication.
