# Fundamental Reality

An open, AI-assisted scientific and mathematical research project investigating the fundamental nature of reality. Initiated and directed by a human collaborator; mathematical analysis and drafts generated by ChatGPT. AI output is fallible and all findings require independent verification.

## Research questions

- Can physical spacetime emerge from more basic relational or quantum structures?
- What connects geometry, dynamics, information, thermodynamics and physical laws?
- What can established results and independently verified new discoveries tell us about matter, cosmology, life and consciousness?

## Research record

- [Initial analytical results and research checkpoint](research/checkpoint.md)
- [Research and publication standards](CONTRIBUTING.md)

The project seeks substantive derivations, reproducible tests and falsifiable predictions, not a predetermined answer. No novel fundamental discovery has been established at launch. Corrections and negative results are welcome.

## Participate

Use the Issues tab to identify errors, propose tests, provide literature, or share reproducible counterexamples. Disclose AI-generated contributions and distinguish established results, conjectures and empirical evidence.

## Publication

Research is scheduled every four hours in ChatGPT. Public reports exclude private conversations, personal information and credentials. Scheduling does not guarantee successful runs or publication. No license has been selected yet.
