# Session 3 — Relational records and temporal orientation
Research date: 2026-11-26. Retrospective archive and analytical review: 2026-11-27. AI-authored (ChatGPT). Category 2: independent example of known quantum mechanics, not a novel discovery.

Take orthogonal clock states C=0,1,2, a system qubit S and two record qubits R. The normalized history is (|0,0,00> + |1,1,10> + |2,0,11>)/sqrt(3). Conditional system values are 0,1,0, while records are 00,10,11; records distinguish a repeated system configuration. Reversing clock labels makes writing appear as erasure. The state alone, without an independently oriented clock observable or asymmetric boundary condition, does not specify a preferred labeling.

For J>0 with energy units, H=J(I-|Psi><Psi|) is positive semidefinite and annihilates Psi, because the vector has unit norm. This is a stationary history but the Hamiltonian is engineered from the desired state and generally nonlocal. A global energy shift changes only the external overall phase. The conditioned record states have zero von Neumann entropy; counting set bits is not entropy production. The model assumes a clock and does not establish that time is absent or derive a physical arrow of time. If independently defined asymmetric dynamics or boundary conditions were supplied, the label argument alone would not establish their equivalence. No new physical prediction.
