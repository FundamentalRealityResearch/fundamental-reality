## Question and model
Can replacing the global projector in Session 3 with nearest-neighbor clock propagation and few-qubit gates select a temporal orientation? Let C be a four-state clock, S and R qubits, J>0 an energy, and U_0=X_S, U_1=CNOT_{S->R}, U_2=X_S. Let v_0=|00>, v_{t+1}=U_t v_t; these yield |00>, |10>, |11>, |01>. Define |Psi>=1/2 sum_{t=0}^3 |t> v_t. All four terms are orthogonal, so its norm is one.

H_prop = (J/2) sum_{t=0}^2 [ (|t><t|+|t+1><t+1|) tensor I - |t+1><t| tensor U_t - |t><t+1| tensor U_t^dagger ].
Each term has nonnegative quadratic form J/2 ||w_{t+1}-U_t w_t||^2 for any state with clock components w_t. Thus H_prop is Hermitian, positive semidefinite, and annihilates Psi. Clock adjacency and gate support are imposed; a one-excitation four-site encoding realizes the clock hopping on adjacent sites, with interaction support on those sites and one or two data qubits. This is not a demonstrated geometrically local relativistic Hamiltonian. All operator factors are dimensionless apart from J. Adding a scalar energy changes external phase, not the ray or correlations.

Let V_t=U_{t-1}...U_0, V_0=I, W=sum |t><t| tensor V_t. Then W^dagger H_prop W=(J/2)L_path tensor I, where L_path is the graph Laplacian of the four-node path. Eigenvalues are J[1-cos(k*pi/4)], k=0,1,2,3, each fourfold degenerate. The zero kernel therefore has dimension four: propagation does not select the initial data state. Add H_in=J |0><0| tensor (I-|00><00|). Both terms are PSD and their common kernel is one-dimensional, since the boundary forces v_0 proportional to |00>. The selection is imposed at a named endpoint, not derived.

Define P|t>=|3-t>. The pair (H',Psi')=(P H_prop P^dagger, P Psi), with identity on data understood, has the same spectrum and stationary relation. Reversed gates are U_{2-t}^dagger. In this example the gates are real, self-inverse and palindromic, so H'=H_prop; reversing the clock alone maps one zero-energy history into another. H_in is instead mapped to the opposite endpoint, so a specified boundary distinguishes them. This is not a claim that every Hamiltonian has this reversal symmetry. In the limit J=0 the propagation imposes no constraint, and the kernel dimension becomes 16.

## Records and entropy
Clock readings 0,1,2,3 give (S,R)=(0,0),(1,0),(1,1),(0,1). System recurrence is resolved by R, but reversed clock readings turn writing into erasure. Each conditional S,R state is pure, so its entropy is zero. Measuring C,S,R in the stated basis gives four equiprobable outcomes. R and S are independent fair bits (each marginal entropy 1 bit; joint entropy 2 bits); I(S:R)=0, I(C:R)=I(C:S)=1 bit. These are classical measurement statistics, not a claim about quantum mutual information of the coherent C-S or C-R marginals. No monotonic thermodynamic entropy is derived.

## Strongest counterargument / falsification
This is a history-state model in standard quantum mechanics (baseline), not a microscopic derivation of time, geometry or entropy. The boundary term can distinguish endpoints, but assumes a particular blank input at a named clock position; that is exactly the asymmetry the research is trying to explain. Real physical time reversal can transform observables and antiunitarily conjugate states; clock relabeling alone is not general microscopic time reversal. The restricted claim would be invalidated by failure of the PSD factorization, spectral calculation, or reversed zero-energy relation under the specified assumptions. The broad idea that records never select an orientation is not established: externally oriented dynamics or asymmetric boundary conditions may do so. No candidate prediction differs from standard quantum mechanics.
